# Kincard Privacy Policy

Last updated: 2026-10-24

Kincard ("the app") is made by Mustafa Alp Yılmaz.

## Data we collect
We do not collect any personal data. Kincard has no server of its own, no account and no analytics. We cannot see your books or your contacts.


## Shared books and iCloud
A book's cards are stored in your iCloud account through Apple's CloudKit. A card holds the name, company, job title, phone numbers, email addresses, postal addresses, birthday and photo.

When you invite someone to a book, Apple shares that book's cards with them through iCloud. Only the people you invite can see them.
Notes on a card and every other field never leave your iPhone. Contacts outside your books are never shared.
Apple's privacy policy applies to iCloud: https://www.apple.com/legal/privacy/

## Purchases
Purchases are processed by Apple. We do not receive your payment details. Apple's privacy policy applies to purchases: https://www.apple.com/legal/privacy/

## Data on your device
Kincard keeps a copy of your books and cards on your iPhone, so it works without a network. Deleting the app removes this copy.
The cards in your Contacts app stay there after you delete the app. They are your contacts.

## Deleting your data
If you own a book, delete it in Kincard: tap the row of people at the top of the book, then Delete. This removes the book from iCloud and ends sharing. Everyone keeps the cards as their own contacts.
If you joined a book, leave it in Kincard. You can keep its cards or remove them from your iPhone.

## Permissions
- Contacts: Kincard adds each book as a group in your Contacts app and keeps its cards up to date. It reads your contacts to list them for import and to match cards when you join a book. Only the cards in a book are shared.
- Background updates: iCloud tells Kincard when a book changes, so it can fetch the change. Kincard shows no notifications.

## Third parties
The app does not include third-party analytics, advertising or tracking. Apple stores the shared books in iCloud.

## Children
The app is not directed at children and does not knowingly collect data from anyone.
