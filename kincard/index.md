# Kincard

Kincard is an iPhone app that keeps one shared address book for a couple or family, in each person's own Contacts app.

Two people with separate Apple Accounts can share notes, reminders and calendars, but not contacts. Couples send each other vCards that never update, or set up a third iCloud account that overwrites their own card. With Kincard, each person keeps their own Apple Account, and both phones list the same plumber, school and relatives.

## What it does
- Shares a book of contacts, such as Family, with the people you invite by link.
- Puts the book's cards into a group in each person's Contacts app, so caller ID shows the right name.
- Sends an edit or a deleted card to everyone in the book.
- Picks up edits made to a book card in the Contacts app the next time you open Kincard.
- Imports cards you already have into a book.
- When you join a book, matches it against cards you already have instead of adding second copies.
- Keeps notes and your other contacts on your iPhone.
- Free: one book shared with one other person, with up to 25 cards.
- Kincard Pro, a one-time purchase of $3.99 in the US: unlimited cards and people, and several books such as Home services and School. The owner buys Pro, and the people they invite join free.

## Works with
Kincard works with the Contacts app on iPhone. The cards reach each person's Mac and iPad through their own iCloud contacts. Sharing runs on iCloud. Kincard is not made by or affiliated with Apple.

## What it does not do
- It does not run on Android or on the web.
- It does not share your personal contacts or your notes.
- It does not sync Google or Exchange accounts.
- It keeps no edit history, so a deleted card cannot be brought back.

## Requirements
iOS 26 or later on iPhone. Each person needs their own Apple Account signed in to iCloud. Kincard needs Full Access to Contacts.

## Questions
**Can my wife and I share contacts with separate Apple Accounts?**
Yes. Kincard shares a book through iCloud, and each of you keeps your own Apple Account.

**Do the shared contacts show up in the Contacts app?**
Yes. Kincard puts each book into a group in everyone's Contacts app, so caller ID shows the shared names.

**Will Kincard share all of my contacts?**
No. Kincard shares only the cards in a book. Your other contacts and every card's notes stay on your iPhone.

**Does my partner have to pay too?**
No. The book owner buys Kincard Pro once. The people they invite join free.

**Do the shared contacts reach my Mac?**
Yes. Kincard writes them to your iPhone's contacts, and iCloud carries them to your Mac and iPad.

**Can I leave a book later?**
Yes. When you leave a book, you choose to keep its cards or remove them. When you delete a book you own, everyone keeps its cards as their own contacts.
