# Kincard Support

Kincard is made by Mustafa Alp Yılmaz. Need help or found a problem? Email mustafalpyilmaz0@gmail.com and you'll get a reply within two business days.

Kincard needs iOS 26 or later on iPhone.

## Common questions

**How do I restore my purchase?**
Tap the book's name at the top, then New book. The Kincard Pro screen opens. Tap Restore purchase. Use the same Apple Account you bought with.

**How do I invite my partner?**
Tap the row of people at the top of the book, then Invite someone. Send the link in Messages or Mail. Your partner opens it on their iPhone and taps Join.

**Why don't the shared cards show up in my Contacts app?**
Kincard needs Full Access to Contacts. Open Settings, Apps, Kincard, Contacts, and choose Full Access. Each book is a group with the book's name in the Contacts app.

**The invite says it no longer works. What now?**
Ask the book owner for a new invite. Each person also needs to be signed in to iCloud on their iPhone.

**How do I get a refund?**
Refunds are handled by Apple: https://reportaproblem.apple.com

**Where is my data stored?**
In your iCloud account and on your iPhone. Kincard has no server of its own. See the privacy policy: https://mustafalpyilmaz.github.io/studio-legal/kincard/privacy-policy.html
