# Ferrydisk Support

Need help or found a problem? Email mustafalpyilmaz0@gmail.com and you'll get a reply within two business days.

## Common questions

**How do I enter my license key?**
Open Ferrydisk > Settings and choose License. Paste the key from the email and click Enter.
The key arrives by email within 24 hours of payment. If you lose it, email us from the address you bought with.

**How do I get a refund?**
Paddle handles payments and refunds. Reply to your Paddle receipt email, or request a refund at https://paddle.net

**Where is my data stored?**
On your device. See the privacy policy: https://mustafalpyilmaz.github.io/studio-legal/ferrydisk/privacy-policy.html
