# Ferrydisk Privacy Policy

Last updated: 2026-10-11

Ferrydisk ("the app") is made by Mustafa Alp Yılmaz.

## Data we collect
The app itself collects no personal data and sends none to us. Your disks, passwords and copies stay on your Mac.
If you buy Ferrydisk Pro, Paddle sends us your name, email address and order details. The Purchases section says how we use them.

## Purchases
Ferrydisk Pro is sold through Paddle (paddle.com), the merchant of record. Paddle processes your payment, and we do not receive your payment details.
Paddle sends us your name, email address and order details. We use them to email you a license key and to answer support requests.
Paddle's privacy policy applies to purchases: https://www.paddle.com/legal/privacy

## Updates
The app checks for updates with Sparkle. It downloads a list of versions from GitHub Pages.
That request sends your IP address and the app's version to GitHub. GitHub's privacy statement applies: https://docs.github.com/site-policy/privacy-policies/github-general-privacy-statement

## Data on your device
For each unfinished copy, the app keeps a progress file in `~/Library/Application Support/Ferrydisk/Jobs`.
The file holds the volume and drive names, the location of the disk image and how far the copy got. It never holds the disk password or encryption keys.
The app deletes a copy's file when the copy finishes or when you discard it. Your license key is kept in your login Keychain.

## Permissions
Ferrydisk reads encrypted disks through a helper that runs as root. The helper reads the source disk and never writes to it.
You allow the helper in System Settings > General > Login Items & Extensions, where it is listed as "Ferrydisk".
The helper answers only Ferrydisk. To remove it, turn it off there or delete the app.

## Third parties
The app does not include third-party analytics, advertising or tracking.

## Children
The app is not directed at children, and we do not knowingly receive data about children.
