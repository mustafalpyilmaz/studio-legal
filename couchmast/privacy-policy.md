# Couchmast Privacy Policy

Last updated: 2026-10-24

Couchmast ("the app") is made by Mustafa Alp Yılmaz.

## Data we collect
We do not collect any personal data. Couchmast has no server of its own, no account and no analytics. We cannot see what you watch.

## Your box
Couchmast talks to your own DVR box over its local interface. Live TV, recordings and the guide go from your box to your device and through no other server.
Away from home, video goes through the VPN you run, such as Tailscale or WireGuard. Couchmast does not set up or run the VPN.

## Finding your box
To find your box, Couchmast asks the box maker's lookup service which boxes share your home's public internet address. The service answers with the boxes' addresses on your home network.
Couchmast also asks each address on your Wi-Fi network for a box on port 8885. Nothing else leaves your device.

## Purchases
Purchases are processed by Apple. We do not receive your payment details. Apple's privacy policy applies to purchases: https://www.apple.com/legal/privacy/

## Data on your device
Couchmast keeps three things on your device: the box you connected, your saved away address, and where you stopped in each recording.
Deleting the app removes them. Recordings and schedules stay on your box.

## Permissions
- Local Network: Couchmast finds your DVR box on your home network and plays its channels. Without it, the app cannot reach the box.

## Support emails
If a box answers in a way Couchmast cannot read, the app offers to email support. The email names the box model, its firmware and the failed request. It holds nothing about what you watch. You see the email before you send it.

## Third parties
The app does not include third-party analytics, advertising or tracking.

## Children
The app is not directed at children and does not knowingly collect data from anyone.

## Changes
If this policy changes, the new version will be posted at this address with a new date.
