# Couchmast Support

Couchmast is made by Mustafa Alp Yılmaz. Email mustafalpyilmaz0@gmail.com and you'll get a reply within two business days.
Couchmast needs iOS 26 or later on iPhone or iPad.

## Common questions

**Which boxes does Couchmast work with?**
Legacy Tablo DVRs: DUAL, DUAL LITE, QUAD and the original 2- and 4-tuner models. Couchmast does not work with 4th-gen Tablo boxes.
Couchmast is not affiliated with or endorsed by Nuvyyo Inc. Tablo is a trademark of Nuvyyo Inc.

**Couchmast does not find my box.**
Check that the box is on and on the same Wi-Fi as your iPhone or iPad. Check that Local Network is on for Couchmast in the Settings app.
If the search still finds nothing, enter the box's address on the No box found screen. Your router app lists the address of each device on your network.

**How do I watch away from home?**
Away from home is part of Couchmast Pro. It plays your box through a VPN you run. Couchmast does not set up or run the VPN.

With Tailscale:
1. Install Tailscale on a computer at home that stays on, and on your iPhone or iPad.
2. On the home computer, turn on subnet routing for your home network, such as 192.168.1.0/24.
3. Approve the route in the Tailscale admin console.
4. Turn on Tailscale on your iPhone or iPad, then open Settings, Away from home in Couchmast and tap Check connection.

With WireGuard:
1. Turn on the WireGuard server in your router, or set one up on a computer at home.
2. Add a peer for your iPhone or iPad. Include your home network, such as 192.168.1.0/24, in its allowed IPs.
3. Import the peer's config into the WireGuard app on your iPhone or iPad.
4. Turn on the tunnel, then open Settings, Away from home in Couchmast and tap Check connection.

**Can I download or export recordings?**
No. Couchmast plays recordings from your box. It does not download or export them.

**How do I restore my purchase?**
Open Settings in Couchmast and tap Restore purchase under Couchmast Pro. Use the same Apple Account you bought with.

**How do I get a refund?**
Refunds are handled by Apple: https://reportaproblem.apple.com

**Where is my data stored?**
On your device and on your box. See the privacy policy: https://mustafalpyilmaz.github.io/studio-legal/couchmast/privacy-policy.html
