# Linetake Support

For help or to report a problem, email mustafalpyilmaz0@gmail.com. Replies come within two business days.

## Common questions

**How do I restore my purchase?**
Choose Linetake > Unlock Pro…, then click Restore Purchase. You can also open Linetake > Settings… and click Unlock Pro…. Use the same Apple Account you bought with.

**Linetake says it can’t use the microphone. What do I do?**
Open System Settings > Privacy & Security > Microphone and turn on Linetake. The message in the session window clears by itself.

**Which script files does Linetake open?**
CSV, TSV and TXT files, the first sheet of an Excel file, and the first table of a Word file. Each row is one line. Save a Numbers file or a Google Sheet as CSV or Excel first.

**Where are my takes?**
In the folder you chose when you set up the script. File > Show Folder in Finder opens it. With Pro, older takes sit in its "Other takes" folder.

**I moved the folder. Are my takes lost?**
No. The session window asks for the folder again. Choose it, and Linetake reads the takes that are in it.

**How do I get a refund?**
Refunds are handled by Apple: https://reportaproblem.apple.com

**Where is my data stored?**
On your Mac. See the privacy policy: https://mustafalpyilmaz.github.io/studio-legal/linetake/privacy-policy.html
