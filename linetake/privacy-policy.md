# Linetake Privacy Policy

Last updated: 2026-10-30

Linetake ("the app") is made by Mustafa Alp Yılmaz.

## Data we collect
We collect no personal data. We run no server, and nothing you record or open in the app reaches us.

## Microphone
Linetake asks to use the microphone. It records your voice for each script line, only while you record a take or while hands-free mode listens.
The audio is written as WAV files to the folder you choose for each script. It is never sent anywhere.

## Your scripts and takes
Linetake reads the script files you open: CSV, TSV, Excel and Word. It does not change them.
Takes are saved in the folder you choose, next to the script or anywhere you pick. Linetake keeps a link to the script and the folder, so a script opens again after a restart.

## Data on your Mac
Linetake keeps its settings, your recent scripts and each take's number, length and date on your Mac.
Deleting the app and its data removes them. Your WAV files stay in their folders until you delete them.

## Purchases
Purchases are processed by Apple. We do not receive your payment details. Apple's privacy policy applies to purchases: https://www.apple.com/legal/privacy/

## Third parties
The app includes no third-party analytics, advertising or tracking.

## Children
The app is not directed at children and does not knowingly collect data from anyone.

## Changes
If this policy changes, the new version will be posted at this address with a new date.
