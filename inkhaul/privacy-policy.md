# Inkhaul Privacy Policy

Last updated: 2026-10-18

Inkhaul ("the app") is made by Mustafa Alp Yılmaz.

## Data we collect
The app does not collect, store on our servers, or share any personal data. Everything you create in the app stays on your device.

## Purchases
Purchases are processed by Apple. We do not receive your payment details. Apple's privacy policy applies to purchases: https://www.apple.com/legal/privacy/

## Data on your device
The app reads notes from the files you choose and uploads nothing.
It saves your export format choice in the app's settings on your Mac.
For the App Store rating prompt, it also counts app launches and finished imports and exports there.
It saves the count of free notes you have used in your Mac's Keychain. This count stays after you delete the app.
Unpacked ZIP files go to a temporary folder that the app deletes when it quits.

## Permissions
The app opens only the files and folders you choose or drop on its window.
It writes imports and exports only to the folder you choose for them.

## Third parties
The app does not include third-party analytics, advertising or tracking.

## Children
The app is not directed at children and does not knowingly collect data from anyone.

## Changes
If this policy changes, the new version will be posted at this address with a new date.
