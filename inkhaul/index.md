# Inkhaul

Inkhaul is a Mac app that moves Samsung Notes into Apple Notes in bulk, for people who keep years of notes in Samsung Notes.

Samsung Notes files (.sdocx) do not open on a Mac or an iPhone. Samsung shares them one note at a time, and handwriting arrives as a flat PDF or not at all. Moving years of notes to Apple Notes means retyping them.

## What it does
- Reads .sdocx files, whole folders of them and .zip archives. Free.
- Lists every note by folder and previews each one as Apple Notes will show it. Free.
- Writes one import file per folder. Apple Notes imports them with the original created and edited dates. The first 10 notes are free.
- Removes the 10-note import limit. Pro.
- Exports notes as PDF or Markdown files, one file per note, in one folder per group. Pro.

Inkhaul Pro is a one-time purchase of $2.99 in the US.

## Works with
- Samsung Notes files (.sdocx), shared from Samsung Notes with Share > Samsung Notes file.
- Typed text with bold, italic, underline and strikethrough.
- Checklists, bulleted and numbered lists, and tables.
- Images and voice recordings, as attachments.
- Handwritten pages with their ink, page images and text boxes, and PDF pages added to a note.
- Apple Notes on the Mac. If Notes uses iCloud, imported notes sync to iPhone and iPad.

Inkhaul is not made by or affiliated with Samsung.

## What it does not do
- It does not draw shapes and lines made with the shape tools.
- It skips locked notes. Unlock them in Samsung Notes first.
- It does not read old S Note (.spd), S Memo (.snb) or Smart Switch backup files.
- It does not turn handwriting into text. Handwritten pages arrive as pictures.
- It does not change your Samsung Notes files. It only reads them.

## Requirements
macOS 26 or later.

## Questions
**Can I transfer Samsung Notes to Apple Notes on a Mac?**
Yes. Inkhaul reads your .sdocx files and writes import files that Apple Notes imports, many notes at once.

**Does Inkhaul keep my handwriting?**
Yes. Inkhaul draws each handwritten page with its ink, and the page arrives in Apple Notes as a picture.

**Will my notes show up on my iPhone?**
Yes. Inkhaul imports into Apple Notes on the Mac. If Notes uses iCloud, the notes sync to your iPhone.

**Does Inkhaul keep my folders?**
Yes. Each folder of notes becomes its own folder in Apple Notes.

**Can I try Inkhaul before I pay?**
Yes. Reading and previewing notes is free, and so is importing the first 10.
