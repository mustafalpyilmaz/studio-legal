Email questions and problems to mustafalpyilmaz0@gmail.com. Replies come within two business days.

## Common questions

**How do I restore my purchase?**
Choose Etchfolio > Unlock Etchfolio Pro…, then click Restore Purchase. Use the same Apple Account you bought with.

**Why don't my files show their design in Finder?**
Finder icons and Quick Look previews of LightBurn and DXF files need Etchfolio Pro. After you unlock it, icons can take a minute to refresh.
If a LightBurn file still shows a plain icon, open it in LightBurn and save it once. LightBurn then stores a preview in the file.

**A folder says "Can't open". What do I do?**
Its drive is not connected, or the folder was deleted. Connect the drive, or click Locate… and choose the folder.

**How do I get a refund?**
Refunds are handled by Apple: https://reportaproblem.apple.com

**Where is my data stored?**
On your Mac. See the privacy policy: https://mustafalpyilmaz.github.io/studio-legal/cutshelf/privacy-policy.html
