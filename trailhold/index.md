# Trailhold

Trailhold is a Mac app that keeps GPX waypoints, routes and tracks in one library for owners of Garmin handhelds and motorcycle navigators.

Garmin BaseCamp gets no Apple silicon version and no more updates, so it stops running when Rosetta goes away. Owners of eTrex, GPSMAP, Oregon and Zumo units keep years of routes and tracks in it. Garmin's web tools cannot import whole exported folders, so hundreds of tracks would need a manual re-import.

## What it does
- Import GPX 1.0 and 1.1 files and whole exported folders. Each folder keeps its name, and each file in it becomes a list. Free.
- A table of every waypoint, route and track, with distance, elevation gain and date. Sort it and search names and notes. Free.
- An Apple map of the selection, in standard or satellite style. Free.
- Edit waypoint names, symbols, notes and positions, with undo. Free.
- Export any list or selection as a GPX file. Free.
- Device transfer: send lists to a mass-storage Garmin unit, browse the files on it and import what it recorded. Pro.
- Route editing: draw a route on the map, then add, move, delete and rename its points. Reverse a route. Pro.
- Track tools: join, split, trim, simplify and reverse tracks, with an elevation profile. Pro.

Trailhold Pro is a one-time purchase of $4.99 in the US.

## Works with
- GPX 1.0 and 1.1 files, including folders exported from BaseCamp.
- Garmin units that mount as a drive, such as the eTrex 30x, GPSMAP 64s, Oregon and Zumo.
- Metric and imperial units, and coordinates in three formats.

Trailhold is not made by or affiliated with Garmin.

## What it does not do
- It cannot reach units that connect only over MTP, such as the eTrex 22x and GPSMAP 66. For those units, it imports and exports GPX files.
- It does not install Garmin maps (.img files) or read BaseCamp's own database. Export from BaseCamp as GPX first.
- It shows no topographic map tiles. The map uses Apple's standard and satellite styles.
- It does not plan routes that follow roads or trails.

## Requirements
macOS 26 or later.

## Questions
**Is there a BaseCamp alternative for Apple silicon Macs?**
Yes. Trailhold runs natively on Apple silicon and imports the GPX folders BaseCamp exports.

**Can I send waypoints and routes to my Garmin from a Mac?**
Yes. With Pro, Trailhold sends lists to Garmin units that mount as a drive, such as the eTrex 30x and Zumo.

**Can Trailhold import a whole folder of GPX files at once?**
Yes. Trailhold imports whole folders. Each folder keeps its name, and each GPX file in it becomes a list.

**Can I edit GPX tracks and routes on a Mac?**
Yes. Trailhold Pro draws and edits routes, and joins, splits, trims and simplifies tracks.

**Does Trailhold work with the eTrex 22x or GPSMAP 66?**
No. Trailhold cannot reach units that connect only over MTP. It imports and exports their GPX files.

**Does Trailhold need an account?**
No. Trailhold keeps your library on your Mac. It has no account and no tracking.
