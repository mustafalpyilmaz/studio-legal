Need help or found a problem? Email mustafalpyilmaz0@gmail.com and you'll get a reply within two business days.

## Common questions

**How do I restore my purchase?**
Choose Trailhold > Unlock Trailhold Pro…, then click Restore Purchase. The same window opens when you use a Pro feature. Use the same Apple Account you bought with.

**How do I get a refund?**
Refunds are handled by Apple: https://reportaproblem.apple.com

**Where is my data stored?**
On your device. See the privacy policy: https://mustafalpyilmaz.github.io/studio-legal/trailhold/privacy-policy.html
