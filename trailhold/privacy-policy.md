# Trailhold Privacy Policy

Last updated: 2026-10-09

Trailhold ("the app") is made by Mustafa Alp Yılmaz.

## Data we collect
The app does not collect, store on our servers, or share any personal data. Everything you create in the app stays on your device.

## Purchases
Purchases are processed by Apple. We do not receive your payment details. Apple's privacy policy applies to purchases: https://www.apple.com/legal/privacy/

## Data on your device
The app keeps these on your Mac, inside its own container:
- Your library: folders, lists, waypoints, routes and tracks you import or create.
- The access you gave to each Garmin unit's drive, so you pick the drive only once.
- Your settings: distance units, coordinate format, map style, table sort and the last map area.

Deleting the app and its container removes them. Files you export or send to a Garmin unit stay where you put them.

## Permissions
The app asks for no system permissions. It reads and writes only the files, folders and Garmin drives you pick in an Open or Save window.

## Maps
The map comes from Apple Maps. To draw it, the app requests map images for the area on screen from Apple. Your library is not sent. Apple's privacy policy applies: https://www.apple.com/legal/privacy/

## Third parties
The app does not include third-party analytics, advertising or tracking.

## Children
The app is not directed at children and does not knowingly collect data from anyone.

## Changes
If this policy changes, the new version will be posted at this address with a new date.
