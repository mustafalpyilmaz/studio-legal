# Echocask Support

Echocask is made by Mustafa Alp Yılmaz. Need help or found a problem? Email mustafalpyilmaz0@gmail.com and you'll get a reply within two business days.

Echocask needs macOS 26 or later.

## Common questions

**How do I restore my purchase?**
Choose Echocask > Unlock Pro…, then click Restore Purchase. Use the same Apple Account you bought with.

**Why does Echocask ask me to choose the Recordings folder?**
macOS lets Echocask read Voice Memos only after you choose its Recordings folder once. The folder window opens at the right place, so click Open. Echocask only reads this folder.

**A recording says "Waiting for download". What do I do?**
The recording is in iCloud but not on this Mac. Open it in Voice Memos on this Mac to download it. Echocask can archive it once it is downloaded.

**A recording says "Deleted in Voice Memos". Can I still keep it?**
Yes. Voice Memos keeps a deleted recording in Recently Deleted for 30 days. Echocask shows the days left, and you can archive it until then.

**Where are my archived recordings?**
In the archive folder you chose, such as Music > Echocask. Choose Recording > Open Archive Folder to see them in Finder.

**How do I get a refund?**
Refunds are handled by Apple: https://reportaproblem.apple.com

**Where is my data stored?**
On your Mac. See the privacy policy: https://mustafalpyilmaz.github.io/studio-legal/echocask/privacy-policy.html
