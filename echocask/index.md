# Echocask

Echocask is a Mac app that backs up Voice Memos recordings with their dates and titles, for people who record lectures, interviews and family voices.

Voice Memos syncs through iCloud, so deleting a recording to free iCloud space deletes it on every device. Exported memos also get the export date instead of the recording date. People have lost lectures and recordings of a parent's voice this way.

## What it does
- Copies each recording to a folder you choose, such as Music > Echocask. Free for up to 20 recordings.
- Names each file by its recording date and title, such as "2026-10-24 12.55 Lecture 3.m4a", and sets the file date to the recording date.
- Copies the .m4a file byte for byte. Nothing is converted.
- Shows every recording on a timeline by day, with its time, length and a waveform. Free.
- Marks each recording Archived, Not archived, Waiting for download, Deleted in Voice Memos, or Only in Echocask. Free.
- Shows the days left for a recording deleted in Voice Memos, so you can archive it before Voice Memos erases it. Free.
- Plays any recording, searches titles and shows archived files in Finder. Free.
- Renames an archived file when you rename the recording in Voice Memos.
- Pro, a one-time purchase of $2.99 in the US, adds:
  - Archive every recording, with no cap.
  - Auto archive: Echocask stays in the menu bar with its window closed and copies new recordings as they sync. It can open at login.
  - Voice Memos folders kept as subfolders in the archive.

## Works with
Voice Memos on the Mac, including recordings synced from an iPhone or iPad through iCloud. Archived files are standard .m4a audio that plays in Finder, QuickTime Player and Music. Echocask is not made by or affiliated with Apple.

## What it does not do
- It does not make transcripts.
- It does not convert audio to other formats.
- It does not delete, edit or restore recordings in Voice Memos.
- It archives only recordings that are downloaded to this Mac.
- It has no iPhone or iPad version.

## Requirements
macOS 26 or later, on Apple silicon or Intel.

## Questions
**Can I delete Voice Memos from iCloud and keep a copy on my Mac?**
Yes. Echocask copies each recording to a folder on your Mac first. The copy stays when you delete the recording in Voice Memos.

**Can I export Voice Memos with the recording date?**
Yes. Echocask names each file by its recording date and title and sets the file date to the recording date.

**Does Echocask archive new recordings automatically?**
Yes, with Pro. Echocask stays in the menu bar and copies each new recording as it syncs to the Mac.

**Can Echocask save a recording I already deleted in Voice Memos?**
Yes, for 30 days. Voice Memos keeps deleted recordings in Recently Deleted, and Echocask shows the days left.

**Does Echocask make transcripts of my recordings?**
No. Echocask keeps the original audio files only.

**Does Echocask need an account or upload my recordings?**
No. Echocask has no account, and your recordings never leave your Mac.
