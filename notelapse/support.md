# Notelapse Support

Notelapse is made by Mustafa Alp Yılmaz. Need help or found a problem? Email mustafalpyilmaz0@gmail.com and you'll get a reply within two business days.

Notelapse needs macOS 26 or later.

## Common questions

**How do I restore my purchase?**
Click the Notelapse icon in the menu bar and choose Settings…. Click Unlock Pro…, then click Restore Purchase. Use the same Apple Account you bought with.

**Why are no new versions saved?**
Notelapse saves versions only while Notes is open. If the menu reads "Waiting for Notes", choose Open Notes. If it reads "Paused", choose Resume Saving.

**Notelapse says it can't read Notes. What do I do?**
Open System Settings > Privacy & Security > Automation. Under Notelapse, turn on Notes, then come back to the Notelapse window.

**How do I get a refund?**
Refunds are handled by Apple: https://reportaproblem.apple.com

**Where is my data stored?**
On your Mac. See the privacy policy: https://mustafalpyilmaz.github.io/studio-legal/notelapse/privacy-policy.html
