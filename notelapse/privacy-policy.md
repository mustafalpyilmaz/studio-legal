# Notelapse Privacy Policy

Last updated: 2026-10-24

Notelapse ("the app") is made by Mustafa Alp Yılmaz.

## Data we collect
The app does not collect, store on our servers, or share any personal data. Everything the app saves stays on your Mac.

## Purchases
Purchases are processed by Apple. We do not receive your payment details. Apple's privacy policy applies to purchases: https://www.apple.com/legal/privacy/

## Data on your device
The app saves the title, folder and text of your notes in Apple Notes, one version each time a note changes. It also keeps its settings. Both stay in the app's own folder on your Mac. Settings > Delete All History removes every saved version.

## Permissions
- Automation for Notes: the app asks Notes for each note's change date and reads the text of notes that changed. A restore asks Notes to create a new note. The app never reads locked notes. You can turn this off in System Settings > Privacy & Security > Automation.
- Login item: if you turn on Open at login in setup or Settings, the app opens at login, so versions keep saving. You can turn it off in the app's Settings.

## Third parties
The app does not include third-party analytics, advertising or tracking.

## Children
The app is not directed at children and does not knowingly collect data from anyone.

If this policy changes, the new version will be posted at this address with a new date.
