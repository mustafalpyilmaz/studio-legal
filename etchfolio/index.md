# Etchfolio

Etchfolio is a Mac app that shows LightBurn, SVG, DXF, PDF and AI files as thumbnails for laser engravers.

On a Mac, LightBurn and DXF files show a plain icon in Finder. To find a design, you open one file after another in LightBurn. LightBurn draws thumbnails on Windows, but not on a Mac.

## What it does
- A thumbnail grid of every LightBurn (.lbrn, .lbrn2), SVG, DXF, PDF and AI file in the folders you add, subfolders included. Free.
- A Kind filter, sorting by date or name, and three card sizes. Free.
- A large preview of any design, with the layer names and modes of each LightBurn file. Free.
- Open in LightBurn, Open With Default App and Show in Finder. Free.
- Finder icons and Quick Look previews for LightBurn and DXF files. Pro.
- PDF catalogs of any folder, 12 designs per page, on US Letter or A4. Pro.
- Cut settings: the speed, power and passes of each LightBurn layer, and the file's notes. Pro.

Etchfolio Pro is a one-time purchase of $2.99 in the US.

## Works with
- LightBurn files (.lbrn and .lbrn2). Etchfolio shows the preview image LightBurn saves in each file.
- SVG, PDF and AI files, drawn by macOS. PDF and AI files show their first page.
- DXF files, drawn by Etchfolio: lines, arcs, circles, polylines, splines and blocks.
- Folders on external drives. They come back when the drive is connected.

Etchfolio is not made by or affiliated with LightBurn Software.

## What it does not do
- It does not edit or save design files. It only reads them.
- It does not draw text or hatches in DXF files.
- A LightBurn file saved without a preview image shows a placeholder. Save it once in LightBurn to add one.
- It does not run on Windows or iPad.

## Requirements
macOS 26 or later.

## Questions
**Can I see LightBurn file thumbnails in Finder on a Mac?**
Yes. Etchfolio Pro adds Finder icons and Quick Look previews for .lbrn and .lbrn2 files.

**Can I browse a folder of LightBurn files visually on a Mac?**
Yes. Etchfolio shows every LightBurn file in a folder as a thumbnail, for free.

**Does Etchfolio show the speed and power of each layer?**
Yes. With Pro, the large preview shows the speed, power and passes saved in each LightBurn layer.

**Can I print a catalog of my designs?**
Yes. With Pro, Etchfolio saves any folder as a PDF catalog with 12 designs per page.

**Does Etchfolio change my files?**
No. Etchfolio only reads your files. It needs no account and has no tracking.
