# Facekeep Privacy Policy

Last updated: 2026-10-24

Facekeep ("the app") is made by Mustafa Alp Yılmaz.

## Data we collect
The app itself collects no personal data and sends none to us. Your photos, face names and snapshots stay on your Mac.
If you buy Facekeep Pro, Paddle sends us your name, email address and order details. The Purchases section says how we use them.

## Purchases
Facekeep Pro is sold through Paddle (paddle.com), the merchant of record. Paddle processes your payment, and we do not receive your payment details.
Paddle sends us your name, email address and order details. We use them to email you a license key and to answer support requests.
Paddle's privacy policy applies to purchases: https://www.paddle.com/legal/privacy

## Updates
The app checks for updates with Sparkle. It downloads a list of versions from GitHub Pages.
That request sends your IP address and the app's version to GitHub. GitHub's privacy statement applies: https://docs.github.com/site-policy/privacy-policies/github-general-privacy-statement

## Data on your device
The app reads the Photos library database read-only, from a temporary copy that it deletes after each read.
It keeps snapshots in `~/Library/Application Support/Facekeep/Libraries`, one folder per library, and the 12 newest snapshots per library.
A snapshot holds each named person's name and ID, and each named face's photo ID and position. It holds no photos.

Settings > General > Show in Finder opens the folder. Delete the folder to remove the snapshots.
Your license key is kept in your login Keychain. The app's settings are kept in its preferences file.

## Permissions
Full Disk Access lets the app read the Photos library database. You turn it on in System Settings > Privacy & Security > Full Disk Access.
Automation lets the app control Photos to add name keywords and make the re-tag album. macOS asks once, at the first Pro run.
You can turn either off in System Settings > Privacy & Security.

## Third parties
The app does not include third-party analytics, advertising or tracking.

## Children
The app is not directed at children, and we do not knowingly receive data about children.
