# Facekeep Support

Facekeep is made by Mustafa Alp Yılmaz. It needs macOS 26 or later.

Need help or found a problem? Email mustafalpyilmaz0@gmail.com and you'll get a reply within two business days.

## Common questions

**How do I enter my license key?**
Open Facekeep > Settings and choose License. Paste the key from the email and click Enter.
You can also click Add Keywords and paste the key under "Already bought it?".
The key arrives by email within 24 hours of payment. If you lose it, email us from the address you bought with.

**How do I get a refund?**
Paddle handles payments and refunds. Reply to your Paddle receipt email, or request a refund at https://paddle.net

**Facekeep says it needs Full Disk Access, but I turned it on.**
Quit Facekeep and open it again. If Facekeep is not in the Full Disk Access list, click + and choose it in Applications.

**Facekeep says it cannot control Photos.**
Open System Settings > Privacy & Security > Automation. Turn on Photos under Facekeep, then click Try Again.

**Where is my data stored?**
On your Mac. See the privacy policy: https://mustafalpyilmaz.github.io/studio-legal/facekeep/privacy-policy.html
