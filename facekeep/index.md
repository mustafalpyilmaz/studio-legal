# Facekeep: Face Name Backup

Facekeep is a Mac app that saves every face name in Apple Photos and shows which names a macOS update or a sync dropped. Pro copies those people tags into keywords on the photos.

Names typed onto faces in Photos can vanish after a macOS update, a rescan or an iCloud sync. Family archivists lose names on thousands of scanned photos and re-tag them by hand. The usual advice is to copy each name into a keyword yourself.

## What it does
- Saves a snapshot of every named face in your Photos library: the person, the photo and the face position. Free.
- Checks Photos against the snapshot each time it opens, and counts lost names per person. Free.
- Shows each person's faces, with lost faces outlined in rose. Free.
- Adds each person's name as a keyword on every photo of them, through Photos. Pro.
- Makes one Photos album of the photos that lost a name, with a "Re-tag" keyword that names who to tag. Pro.

Facekeep Pro is a one-time purchase of $2.99 in the US. The license key arrives by email within 24 hours of payment.

## Works with
- Apple Photos libraries on macOS 26, including libraries on an external drive.
- Name keywords work in Photos search and in smart albums with "Keyword is".
- Facekeep reads the Photos database read-only. It changes Photos only through Photos itself: keywords and one album.

Facekeep is not made by or affiliated with Apple.

## What it does not do
- It does not write face names back. No Apple tool lets an app do that, so you re-tag lost faces in Photos.
- It does not remove keywords you added, and it never changes a face.
- It does not read iCloud Shared Photo Library photos, only your personal library.
- It does not check in the background. It checks when you open it.

## Requirements
macOS 26 or later, on Apple silicon or Intel.
Facekeep needs Full Disk Access to read the Photos library. Pro features ask once to let Facekeep control Photos.

## Questions
**Can I back up the face names in Apple Photos?**
Yes. Facekeep saves every named face in a snapshot on your Mac, and keeps the 12 newest snapshots per library.

**Can I find out which names disappeared after a macOS update?**
Yes. Facekeep compares Photos with the snapshot each time it opens and lists each person whose faces lost their name.

**Can Facekeep put the names back on the faces?**
No. No Apple tool lets an app name a face. Facekeep makes an album of the photos to re-tag and adds each name as a keyword, which Photos keeps.

**Do I need to copy names into keywords by hand?**
No. With Pro, click Add Keywords and pick all people. Facekeep writes each name as a keyword on that person's photos.

**Does Facekeep change my Photos library?**
No for faces. Facekeep only reads the Photos database. With Pro it adds keywords and one album through Photos.
