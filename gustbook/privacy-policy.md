# Gustbook Privacy Policy

Last updated: 2026-10-10

Gustbook ("the app") is made by Mustafa Alp Yılmaz.

## Data we collect
We collect no personal data. We run no server, and nothing the app records reaches us.

## Weather readings
Gustbook receives the readings your AcuRite Access hub sends and stores them on your Mac.
Readings stay on your Mac. Deleting the app and its data removes them.

## Where readings are sent
- **AcuRite.** With "Forward readings to AcuRite" on, the default, Gustbook passes each upload from your hub to AcuRite's server unchanged. AcuRite's privacy policy applies to that data: https://www.acurite.com/privacy-policy. Turn the switch off in Settings > General to keep readings only on your Mac.
- **Weather Underground.** Only if you turn on uploads in Settings > Uploads, Gustbook sends the chosen sensor's readings with your station ID and key. Weather Underground's privacy policy applies.
- **CWOP.** Only if you turn on uploads in Settings > Uploads, Gustbook sends a weather report with your station ID and the position you enter. CWOP publishes these reports.

## Your hub's server name
During setup, Gustbook sets your hub's server name to a name such as `192-168-1-42.sslip.io`.
The hub looks that name up through public DNS, which answers with your Mac's local address. The sslip.io service sees the lookup, not your readings.
You can type your own server name instead. Settings > General > "Point back…" sets the hub back to AcuRite.

## Data on your Mac
Gustbook keeps its readings database, your settings and the Weather Underground station key on your Mac. The key is kept in the Keychain.

## Permissions
Gustbook asks to use the local network, to change your hub's server name and to receive its uploads.

## Purchases
Purchases are processed by Apple. We do not receive your payment details. Apple's privacy policy applies to purchases: https://www.apple.com/legal/privacy/

## Third parties
The app includes no third-party analytics, advertising or tracking.

## Children
The app is not directed at children and does not knowingly collect data from anyone.
