# Gustbook

Gustbook is a Mac app that records the readings of an AcuRite Access hub for home weather station owners.

The AcuRite app keeps 30 days of station history for free, and more history needs a subscription. Weather Underground sharing is paid too. Owners want to keep their own station data without a yearly fee.

## What it does
- Records every upload from your AcuRite Access hub on your Mac, with no limit on history. Free.
- Passes each upload on to AcuRite unchanged, so the AcuRite app keeps working. You can turn this off. Free.
- Reading cells for each sensor: temperature, humidity, dew point, wind, rain today, pressure and UV. Free.
- 24 hour and 7 day charts, and daily records for the last 7 days. Free.
- Imports My AcuRite CSV exports. Free.
- Sensor names, units, and the latest temperature in the menu bar. Free.
- Charts for 30 days, 1 year and every stored day. Pro.
- Daily records for every stored day, sortable by column. Pro.
- Uploads to Weather Underground and CWOP. Pro.
- CSV export of any sensor and date range. Pro.

Gustbook Pro is a one-time purchase of $2.99 in the US.

## Works with
- The AcuRite Access hub, and the Atlas, Iris (5-in-1) and tower sensors it receives.
- CSV files from the Export Data button on My AcuRite.

Gustbook sets the hub's server name, so the hub uploads to your Mac over HTTPS. Settings points the hub back to AcuRite at any time.

Gustbook is not made by or affiliated with AcuRite or Weather Underground.

## What it does not do
- It does not work with the smartHUB, Wi-Fi displays or USB consoles.
- It records only while the Mac is on and Gustbook is running. Turn on Start at login in Settings.
- It does not read Weather Underground or weewx files.
- It does not run on iPhone or Windows.

## Requirements
macOS 26 or later, and an AcuRite Access hub on the same network as the Mac.

## Questions
**Can I keep my AcuRite weather history without a subscription?**
Yes. Gustbook stores every reading from your Access hub on your Mac, with no limit on history and no subscription.

**Does the AcuRite app still work with Gustbook?**
Yes. Gustbook passes each upload on to AcuRite unchanged, unless you turn forwarding off.

**Can I upload my AcuRite station to Weather Underground for free?**
No. Weather Underground and CWOP uploads need Gustbook Pro, a one-time purchase of $2.99 in the US.

**Can I import my My AcuRite history?**
Yes. Gustbook imports the CSV from the Export Data button on My AcuRite, for free.

**Does Gustbook work with the smartHUB?**
No. Gustbook works with the AcuRite Access hub only.

**Does Gustbook collect my data?**
No. Gustbook has no account, no server and no tracking. It sends readings only to AcuRite, Weather Underground and CWOP, while those switches are on.
